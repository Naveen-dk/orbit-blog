          Predicting Abandonment 14 Days Early

          Most goal trackers fail because they only report the past: a missed streak, an uncompleted checkbox, an empty calendar slot. By then, emotional disengagement has already taken root.

          The Orbit DeepMind Engine evaluates multi-variable telemetry—daily check-in sentiment, task completion latency, velocity deltas, and recovery intervals—to forecast drop-off risks up to two weeks in advance.

          Traditional tools tell you when you failed.
          The Orbit DeepMind Engine course-corrects you before you stall.

          How Trajectory Vectors Work

          Every goal is modeled as a multi-dimensional trajectory. Rather than scoring raw hours worked, the model measures vector alignment: did today's actions bring the system closer to the target milestone?

          When the model senses friction, it automatically suggests scope reduction or milestone restructuring via Orbit Copilot.
