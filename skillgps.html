          The Curse of Not Knowing What to Learn Next

          When someone decides to learn a new skill—like modern full-stack development—they are greeted by an overwhelming swamp of tutorials, frameworks, and conflicting roadmaps.

          SkillGPS Foundation structures the learning domain into an acyclic directed knowledge graph. It knows exactly which prerequisite unlocks which capability.

          A goal without a roadmap is just a wish.
          SkillGPS gives every wish a navigational coordinate.
